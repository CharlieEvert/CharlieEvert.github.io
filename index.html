An island, then a jump door.

I grew up on St. Thomas, in the U.S. Virgin Islands — small island, big water, and a season every year that reminds you nothing is guaranteed. You learn to fix what is in front of you with what you have.

In 2015 I enlisted as an airborne infantryman, 11B, at Fort Benning, Georgia. Airborne infantry taught me to face fear, give the work my full effort, and take responsibility for the people beside me. At the jump door, that becomes very concrete: rehearse, check your equipment, trust your training, and go when the light turns green. An honorable medical discharge for injury ended that in 2016 — but the operating instinct stayed. Rehearse with evaluations, put a human where confidence is low, then actually jump instead of running another pilot.

01

A $5M engagement: from discovery to business impact

During discovery on an existing engagement, I found a client team spending over $1M a year manually tagging calls. Answering “why are calls spiking?” took 1–2 weeks, while knowledge gaps led to email exchanges with no consistent process to resolve them.

I personally originated the sole-source $5M expansion, then sold, staffed, delivered, and invoiced it in 16 weeks at the target 34% margin. Owned the client relationship, SOW, pricing, legal and procurement coordination, staffing and travel budgets, and delivery with seven specialists.

We automated contact-driver tagging and connected proactive alerts with executive self-service questions, reducing call-spike analysis to minutes. Knowledge gaps and article requests entered one workflow: identify subject-matter experts, email targeted questions, draft from their answers, route human review, and publish to Adobe AEM. Call intelligence and documented processes helped leaders decide what to automate next.

Eight of 10 contact-driver staff moved into AI experience roles, representing about $800K in annual capacity. The solution enabled a six-month plan to halve a $10M/year knowledge workload, or $5M in projected annual capacity. I productized the build as a $700K accelerator sold to two more clients through RFPs.

Across the portfolio, including the $5M engagement above, lead US/India project teams up to 20, including Managers, Senior Managers, and a Director as advisory SME. Chair monthly steering committees across two accounts with Chief Data & AI Officer and contact-center leaders to align investment, priorities, risk, and business ownership.
