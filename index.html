01 / Origin

18°20′N 64°56′W
St. Thomas, USVI
→ Fort Benning, GA

An island, then a jump door.

I grew up on St. Thomas, in the U.S. Virgin Islands — small island, big water, and a season every year that reminds you nothing is guaranteed. You learn to fix what is in front of you with what you have.

In 2015 I enlisted as an airborne infantryman, 11B, at Fort Benning, Georgia. Airborne infantry taught me to face fear, give the work my full effort, and take responsibility for the people beside me. At the jump door, that becomes very concrete: rehearse, check your equipment, trust your training, and go when the light turns green. An honorable medical discharge for injury ended that in 2016 — but the operating instinct stayed. Rehearse with evaluations, put a human where confidence is low, then actually jump instead of running another pilot.
