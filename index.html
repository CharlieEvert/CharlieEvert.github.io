01 / Origin

18°20′N 64°56′W
St. Thomas, USVI
→ Fort Benning, GA

An island, then a jump door.

I grew up on St. Thomas, in the U.S. Virgin Islands — small island, big water, and a season every year that reminds you nothing is guaranteed. You learn to fix what is in front of you with what you have.

In 2015 I enlisted as an airborne infantryman, 11B, at Fort Benning, Georgia. Jumping out of an aircraft is mostly checklist and trust: rehearse the thing, check the man in front of you, go when the light turns green. An honorable medical discharge for injury ended that in 2016 — but the operating instinct stayed. Rehearse with evaluations, put a human where confidence is low, then actually jump instead of running another pilot.
