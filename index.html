01:00–02:20 · Why this story matters

Traditional services and pure products are both under pressure. The future is the bridge.

Services firms struggle when they drift from their roots — knowing what everyone is doing and what works. Product firms struggle because AI makes product surfaces easier to copy. The durable model is a balance: proven peer solutions, tailored implementation, client ownership, and a path to the next problem.

Services riskAdvice alone is weaker when everyone can research and generate.
Product riskScreenshots and AI collapse the time to mimic product features.
Winning model“We solved this for your peers — see it, trust it, now own your version.”

Future modelPeer-proven accelerators + tailored delivery + client ownership + next-problem roadmap.

The real problem was advisor-service friction at scale.

The client wanted to reduce service cost, diagnose call spikes faster, improve knowledge coverage for FSPs, and protect advisor trust in an independent-advisor channel vulnerable to digital disruption.

17:10–18:40 · Enterprise AI parallels

The playbook is the same. The blast radius changes.

SIGNAL maps to both definitions of Enterprise AI: internal rewiring with tools like Claude Code / CoWork / ChatGPT, and customer-facing applications with agents. Both need value, governance, adoption, and delivery discipline. The difference is speed and risk.

Internal rewiringFaster iteration; focus on behavior change and productivity telemetry.
Customer-facingHigher brand/compliance risk; evals, HITL, rollback, observability are mandatory.
