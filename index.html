The deep dive · nine moves

01 / 09

The problem

A five-million-dollar manual-labor problem hiding in the service center.

Two onshore teams manually decoded advisor calls and wrote the fixes — ~10 analysts tagging drivers ($1.5–2M/yr) and 30+ knowledge writers ($2–3M/yr), with ~5 days to spot a spike and ~5 more to fix it. A ~$5M annual labor pool.

02 / 09

Success measures

Every metric laddered to cost of service or advisor trust.

80% agent-workflow coverage in two years · 100% of contact drivers mapped to knowledge articles · 20–40% FSP reduction by year two · flat CSAT as the guardrail. The goal was never “add AI.”

03 / 09

Presales

Won the room with a working prototype, not a deck.

Copilot captured stakeholder verbatims, Claude synthesized the North Star, and Claude Code built the first single-page app. The toughest gatekeeper heard her own words in it — multiple EVPs asked to implement it in the same meeting.

04 / 09

The product

SIGNAL: call transcripts become live service intelligence.

Driver trends and spike detection every 15 minutes (proactive) · ask-the-data across semantic search, clustering, and text-to-SQL (reactive) · confidence scores, human review, and knowledge-gap routing (operational).

05 / 09

Architecture

Simple enough to ship, strong enough to govern.

NiCE transcripts → Snowflake → semantic matching in OpenSearch → Claude for summaries and Q&A → gap flagging into the article workflow. AWS-native — S3, WAF, DynamoDB, AgentCore, Dynatrace — with human-in-the-loop review.

06 / 09

Contract & delivery

Sixteen weeks, scoped to reality.

3 discovery, 10 build, 3 test + aftercare with a lean five-person U.S. team. Non-prod first, separate production gate, article authoring descoped. Daily scrums, Friday exec status, monthly SteerCo to the C-suite.

07 / 09

Transformation

Not replacing people — moving them up the stack.

On our recommendation the manual tagging team was repurposed into an AI Experience / Voice-of-Advisor capability: reviewing low-confidence signals, tuning prompts and guardrails, prioritizing gaps from live demand. In daily non-prod use and nearing production.

08 / 09

Expansion

One client win became a repeatable accelerator.

Prebuilt in our AWS environment, SIGNAL de-risked delivery and won new logos — an airline RFP, a bank, plus follow-on personalization work at the original client. Recognized with the Amplify Impact Award — 10 of 36,000.

09 / 09

The operating model

Show it. Sell it. Deliver it. Expand it.

AI as developer, PM, PMO, and sales support — including experiential RFP responses (interactive HTML in the proposal, an inbound phone agent with full RFP context) that moved win rates from roughly 20% to 70%.
