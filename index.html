01 / Origin

18°20′N 64°56′W
St. Thomas, USVI
→ Fort Benning, GA

An island, then a jump door.

I grew up on St. Thomas, in the U.S. Virgin Islands — small island, big water, and a season every year that reminds you nothing is guaranteed. You learn to fix what is in front of you with what you have.

In 2015 I enlisted as an airborne infantryman, 11B, at Fort Benning, Georgia. Jumping out of an aircraft is mostly checklist and trust: rehearse the thing, check the man in front of you, go when the light turns green. An honorable medical discharge for injury ended that in 2016 — but the operating instinct stayed. Rehearse with evaluations, put a human where confidence is low, then actually jump instead of running another pilot.

02 / The turn

2017 — 2023
Accounting → NLP
→ enterprise GenAI

From accounting, to research, to the first GenAI function at a Fortune 100.

A BBA in accounting at Temple taught me to read a business as numbers. An MBA in data analytics and an MS in business intelligence at Saint Joseph’s — 3.85 and 3.95 — taught me to build with them. As a graduate research assistant I co-authored four peer-reviewed papers on NLP and econometrics, running the whole pipeline myself: scraping at scale, embeddings, LDA topic modeling, sentiment, clustering, significance testing.

Then Johnson & Johnson Consumer Health, two months after ChatGPT launched. I joined as a data engineer, built the first prototype of a Teams-based assistant, and by month six was running the company’s new enterprise GenAI function — the capability, the vendor bench, the governance, and the delivery model. The organization later hired a VP of Data Science & AI to lead what had been stood up.
